Who We Are

A National Voice for Locally Led Change

Lim Nguen Foundation (LNF) is a National NGO dedicated to empowering displaced and host communities in South Sudan through sustainable solutions and locally led resilience-building.

Mission: To uplift lives through education in emergencies, community resilience, and locally led humanitarian action that puts affected communities at the center of solutions.

Vision: A South Sudan where every displaced and host community has the tools, resources, and agency to build a resilient, dignified future.

Our Programs

Initiatives that empower communities and build resilience.

Education in Emergencies

Providing safe learning spaces, school rehabilitation, teacher training, and WASH facilities in crisis-affected areas.

  • School rehabilitation
  • Teacher training
  • WASH facilities
Learn more

Resilience Building

Strengthening community capacity to withstand conflict, displacement, and climate shocks through sustainable solutions.

  • Community planning
  • Livelihood support
  • Climate adaptation
Learn more

Locally Led Action

Trusting, resourcing, and empowering local actors who are closest to the crisis to lead humanitarian responses.

  • Local partnerships
  • Institutional strengthening
  • Community ownership
Learn more

Youth Leadership

Engaging young people in climate action, sustainable development, and continental dialogue through ECW membership.

  • Youth forums
  • ECW sub-group
  • Climate webinars
