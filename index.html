Uplifting Lives

Who We Are

A Youth-Led National NGO Building Resilient Communities

Lim Nguen Foundation (LNF) is a National Non-Governmental Organization founded in 2019 and legally registered with the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC) under Chapter III, Section 10 of the NGOs Act 2016, Registration No. 4585. LNF was founded and is led by young South Sudanese with lived experiences of displacement. We work alongside displaced, vulnerable and marginalized communities affected by conflict, climate change, poverty and limited access to essential services through locally led humanitarian, development and resilience-building solutions.

We work with refugees, internally displaced persons, women, young people and vulnerable host communities affected by conflict, displacement, climate change, poverty and limited access to essential services. Our work spans humanitarian response, education, health and WASH, protection and GBV, food security and agriculture, livelihoods, climate resilience, digital inclusion and innovation, peacebuilding, and youth leadership and civic engagement.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To empower refugees, IDPs, women, youth, and vulnerable communities through humanitarian assistance, climate resilience, education, livelihoods, protection, innovation, and community-led development.

What We Do

Our Programs

Nine interconnected thematic areas driving holistic community development.

Humanitarian Response & Emergency Support

Rapid, life-saving assistance to communities affected by conflict, floods, and other emergencies.

  • NFI distribution
  • Cash assistance

Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH)

Ensuring access to clean water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene promotion to prevent disease.

  • Borehole rehabilitation
  • Cholera prevention

Protection & Gender-Based Violence (GBV)

Preventing and responding to GBV, promoting child protection, and ensuring safe spaces for vulnerable groups.

  • TFGBV digital safety
  • Dignity kits

Education, ECDE & Education in Emergencies

Providing safe learning spaces, teacher training, and educational materials for children affected by crisis.

  • School rehabilitation
  • Teacher training

Food Security & Agriculture

Improving agricultural production, promoting nutrition, and building resilience against food insecurity.

  • Farmer field schools
  • Seed distribution

Livelihoods & Entrepreneurship

Empowering youth and women with vocational skills, business training, and market linkages.

  • Skills training
  • Micro-grants

Climate Change, Adaptation & Environmental Resilience

Supporting communities to adapt to climate shocks through environmental conservation and resilient practices.

  • Flood mitigation
  • Tree planting

Youth Leadership & Civic Engagement

Engaging youth in decision-making, advocacy, and community development initiatives.

  • Youth forums
  • Leadership training

Digital Inclusion & Innovation

Bridging the digital divide through ICT access, digital literacy, and innovative solutions for development.

  • Digital safety training
  • Tech hubs
